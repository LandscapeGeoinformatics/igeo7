## DGGS Area and Shape Distortions

**Kmoch, A., Vasilyev, I., Virro, H., Uuemaa, E. (2022).** Area and Shape Distortions in Open-Source Discrete Global Grid Systems. *Big Earth Data*.
[https://doi.org/10.1080/20964471.2022.2094926](https://doi.org/10.1080/20964471.2022.2094926)

Systematic assessment of area and shape distortions across H3, S2, rHEALPix, and other open-source DGGS. Provides the quantitative basis for IGEO7's equal-area motivation.

## Central Place Indexing (Z7 Theory)

**Sahr, K. (2019).** Central Place Indexing: Hierarchical Linear Indexing Systems for Mixed-Aperture Hexagonal Discrete Global Grid Systems. *Cartographica*, 54(1), 16–29.
[https://doi.org/10.3138/cart.54.1.2018-0022](https://doi.org/10.3138/cart.54.1.2018-0022)

The theoretical foundation for the Z7 indexing system — how hierarchical digit encoding and GBT arithmetic underpin the index.

## DGGS Foundations

**Sahr, K., White, D., Kimerling, A.J. (2003).** Geodesic Discrete Global Grid Systems. *Cartography and Geographic Information Science*.
[https://doi.org/10.1559/152304003100011090](https://doi.org/10.1559/152304003100011090)

The canonical reference for DGGS design principles: base polyhedra, projections, cell shapes, and refinement strategies.
